# Lists and tables

This short book shows how the reader handles lists and tables. Watch the left margin during the lists, and the quick grid before each table cell.

## A shopping list

Before the weekend, a few things to buy:

• fresh bread from the bakery
• apples, pears and a lemon
• milk and coffee
• a newspaper for Sunday
• flowers for the table

That was a bulleted list. Each item had its own dot down the left margin, and the current one was lit.

## Getting started

To set up the reader, follow these steps in order:

1. Charge the battery for an hour.
2. Insert the SD card with your books.
3. Hold the button to switch it on.
4. Choose a book from the library.
5. Press play and relax your eyes.

Letters work too:

a) a short pause between items
b) the letter shown in the margin
c) nothing read aloud that isn't text

## Paintings and their cities

A table is read row by row. Before each cell, a small grid shows where you are.

| Year | Painting | City |
|---|---|---|
| 1503 | Mona Lisa | Paris |
| 1498 | The Last Supper | Milan |
| 1486 | The Birth of Venus | Florence |

After the table, the text goes on as usual. That is all there is to it.
